Cost-aware provider choice

FI talks to two billing models. Pick wisely:

Per-call (Copilot) — every LLM call is one "premium request" regardless of token count. Best for: bursty, heavy single quests where a few large prompts dominate.

Per-token (OpenAI / Codex / Claude / Gemini) — prompt and completion tokens are priced separately. Best for: long-running automations where you can keep prompts skinny. Cost charts on each quest show the running total.

Free (Ollama, local) — runs on your machine. Quality varies; use for high-volume cheap nodes or airgapped runs.
