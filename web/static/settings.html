Cost-aware provider choice

FI talks to two billing models. The wrong pick can be a 5–10× cost difference on a long quest.

Per-call

Copilot

Every LLM call is one "premium request" regardless of token count. Best for bursty heavy quests where a few large prompts dominate.

Per-token

OpenAI / Codex / Claude / Gemini

Prompt and completion tokens are priced separately. Best for long-running automations where you can keep prompts skinny. Cost charts on each quest show the running total.

Free

Ollama

Runs on your machine. Quality varies; use for high-volume cheap nodes or airgapped runs.
